About Benjamin

A Personal Approach to Senior Care

I'm Benjamin Mithra. I offer personalized companionship, life coaching and non-clinical caregiving support for older adults.

My approach is built around meaningful conversation, patience, dignity and individual attention. Whether through online interaction or in-person assistance, my aim is to make everyday life more engaging, comfortable and fulfilling.
